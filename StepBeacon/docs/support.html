StepBeacon Support

StepBeacon helps you monitor step progress, walking distance, floors climbed, and momentum trends from your iPhone.

Before contacting support

  • Make sure Motion & Fitness access is enabled for StepBeacon in iPhone Settings.
  • Confirm your device has recent movement data available from Apple motion services.
  • If the app is showing demo data, turn demo mode off in Settings.

Project and issue tracking

The project repository is available at github.com/drsaikrishnathota1/stepbeacon-ios.

For product feedback or bug reports, open an issue at the StepBeacon issue tracker.
